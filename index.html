The Gap

Good projects still go unfunded.

Many energy projects struggle to attract capital — not because of weak technology, but because the investment story is unclear, or misaligned with what investors are mandated to fund. That gap shows up as months of scattered outreach: developers talking to the wrong investors, or the right investors never hearing about the right projects. Northgreen Energy exists to close it, with a clear, structured path from project to financial close.

Our Approach

From promising to financed.

Getting a project funded is rarely a technology problem — it's a sequencing problem: the right story, shown to the right investor, at the right time. We work it in four steps.

01 — Position

Assess the project as an investor would.

Revenue certainty, technology risk, permitting status, offtake structure — we identify what needs to be strengthened before the project goes to market.

02 — Structure

Build the investment narrative.

A clear risk allocation, a credible financial model, and a story that holds up under diligence — built around what capital actually wants to see.

03 — Match

Identify the right investors.

Sector, ticket size, geography, risk appetite — we target the investors and lenders whose mandate genuinely fits, instead of a broad, unfocused search.

04 — Connect

Open the right conversations.

Direct dialogue between developers and the financiers, EPCs, banks and manufacturers who need to be at the table — and we stay involved through to close.

How We Work

One facilitator, the whole value chain.

No single party in an energy project speaks quite the same language as the others — a developer's technical case, a bank's credit criteria, an EPC's delivery risk, an investor's mandate. Northgreen Energy sits at the centre of that conversation, translating between commercial, technical and financial language so every side moves faster, and fewer deals stall on misunderstanding rather than substance.

For developers

Northgreen Energy helps developers prepare projects for capital — sharpening the investment case, structuring the narrative, and connecting them with relevant investors and lenders. That often means stress-testing the financial model, clarifying risk allocation, and reframing the project in the terms institutional capital actually evaluates against.

We stay involved through investor conversations, term sheets and due diligence — not just the introduction.

For investors

Northgreen Energy provides curated access to energy infrastructure opportunities, with direct dialogue with the developers and technical partners behind each project. Every opportunity we bring forward has already been assessed against the fundamentals institutional capital cares about, so time spent in diligence goes further.

We work across BESS, data centers, EV charging and solar — bringing deal flow matched to specific mandates rather than generic pipeline.

Why Northgreen Energy

Facilitators, not brokers.

A broker makes the introduction and moves on to the next deal. We stay in the room — sharpening the investment case, sitting through diligence, working the deal until it's financed, not just introduced. That's also why Northgreen Energy isn't tied to a single bank, technology or developer: independence is what lets us tell a project's story honestly — flagging what needs to be fixed as readily as what's investment-ready — and match it with capital on its merits, not on who we're obligated to favor.
